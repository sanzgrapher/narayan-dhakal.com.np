TRaiFIC is an Automatic Number Plate Recognition pipeline built for **Nepali plates**. Upload a photo or video; three models run in sequence - plate detection, deskew / character segmentation, then OCR for Devanagari glyphs - and you get the reading plus per-character confidence.

The **live demo** runs a lightweight base model so it fits the current hosting limits. For the full power of the trained models, clone the repository, train locally (or on your own GPU), and swap in the weights. A walkthrough video of the system is on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/narayandhakal09_machinelearning-computervision-traific-ugcPost-7323668281288978433-ybpB/).
