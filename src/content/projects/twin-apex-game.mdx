**Modes.** Solo against AI, local two-player on one keyboard, online rooms for up to six, or Free Roam with no rivals, laps or timer.

**Setup.** Choose a circuit (beach Grand Tour, canyon, alpine and more), a car with its own speed / accel / grip feel, and 1–10 laps. Online hosts set the room; everyone else joins with a short code.

**Race.** Drift above ~50 km/h for style, release for a boost. Hold the mirror key for a rear view. After you finish, spectate whoever is still out — or jump straight to results in solo.

**Trained AI.** Solo can run **Classic** bots or a **Trained** library evolved on the same driving physics as the game — every track × car pairing, across styles like racer, drifter, clean, late-braker and rookie. **Level** picks the field: Easy (slower pack, more mistakes), Pro (mixed pace), or Apex (fastest five). An arena view lets you watch the quickest and slowest drivers race any map.

**Ghost.** In Free Roam, turn Ghost on for a standing-start time trial. Your fastest lap per map is saved and replays as a see-through car beside you at GO.

**Extras.** Phones and tablets get on-screen steer, gas, brake, drift, mirror and reset. Pause and restart when you are not online.
