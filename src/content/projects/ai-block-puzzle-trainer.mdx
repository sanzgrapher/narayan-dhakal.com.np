The game itself is a colour block puzzle in the spirit of **1010!**: an 8×8 board, a hand of polyomino shapes (lines, squares, L and T pieces and more), and points for clearing full rows and columns.

On top of that sits an **AI Game Trainer**. Up to fifty agents play at once. A genetic algorithm breeds the next generation from the best scorers, and live charts track peak and average high scores. You can switch between player mode, training, and model compare — including loading champion JSON weights and asking for a Best Move.
