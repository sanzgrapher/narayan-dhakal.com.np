You take over a lower-division club and grow it into a city pride. On **Home**, tap sponsorship deals for cash, watch fans and reputation climb, and buy negotiation upgrades so every tap pays more.

**Squad** is a full starting eleven: filter by GK / DEF / MID / ATT, shuffle or recover fitness, swap only valid replacements, and treat tired players before matchday wages bite.

**Match** runs live. Change between Balanced, Attacking and Defending, track shots and xG on a mini-pitch, and read short commentary while the clock ticks.

**Business** ties the loop together. Stadium seats, concourse shops and merchandise only profit when demand (fans) keeps up with upkeep - win matches, grow the crowd, then expand without going cashflow-negative.

The design and systems were my game plan; the build was vibecoded with coding agents on Next.js.
